Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Bulk Update Pro ("we", "our", or "the App") is a monday.com marketplace application that helps users update multiple items simultaneously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

Bulk Update Pro accesses the following data through the monday.com API:

  • Board Data: Item names, column values, and column configurations from boards where you use the app
  • User Information: User names and IDs within your workspace (for People column functionality)

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any of this data to external servers. All data processing occurs entirely within the monday.com platform.

3. How We Use Your Information

The data accessed is used solely to:

  • Display items and columns in the app interface
  • Execute bulk update operations that you initiate
  • Show user options for People column assignments

4. Data Storage

Bulk Update Pro does NOT store any of your data. The app:

  • Does not have external databases
  • Does not use cookies or local storage for your board data
  • Does not transmit data to third-party services
  • Processes all data in real-time through monday.com's secure API

5. Data Security

Your data security is ensured through:

  • monday.com's OAuth 2.0 authentication
  • monday.com's secure API infrastructure
  • No external data transmission or storage

6. Third-Party Services

Bulk Update Pro does not integrate with or share data with any third-party services. The app operates exclusively within the monday.com ecosystem.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Uninstall the app at any time from your monday.com account
  • Revoke API permissions through monday.com's app management
  • Contact us for any privacy-related questions

8. Children's Privacy

Bulk Update Pro is a business productivity tool and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes through the monday.com marketplace listing.
